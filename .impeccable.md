## Design Context

### Users
Manris v2 is used by ministry teams handling risk and incident management in a government operational context. For incident reporting, the primary user is staff or coordinators capturing a field event while facts are still incomplete and time pressure is high. Their immediate job is to record reliable first facts quickly, then enrich the record with analysis and follow-up actions once the situation is stable.

### Brand Personality
Calm, credible, and decisive. The product should feel institutional without becoming stiff, and supportive without becoming casual. In stressful moments, the interface should reduce panic, establish trust, and help users move step by step.

### Aesthetic Direction
Light-first operational interface with clear hierarchy, restrained color, and strong editorial structure. Avoid startup-dashboard theatrics, decorative AI tropes, and generic admin-card repetition. Incident flows should feel focused, steady, and efficient rather than flashy or experimental.

### Design Principles
1. Prioritize rapid factual capture before deeper analysis.
2. Use hierarchy and spacing to reduce cognitive load under stress.
3. Reserve strong color for meaning, not decoration.
4. Keep copy plain, directive, and human in high-pressure workflows.
5. Treat AI as explicit assistance, never as ornamental chrome.
