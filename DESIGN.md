### Manris Agent

Agent narrative starts with the answer or main finding. Ordinary replies target 1–3 sentences and at most 60 words; when a result component already answers the question, prefer one sentence of interpretation. Do not repeat table/chart/card contents, clarification choices, process narration, metadata blocks or generic follow-up offers. Expand when the user requests detail or accuracy requires explanation. Preserve concise material warnings about incomplete/stale data, failures, access and explicit approval. Mention scope/period when needed to interpret the result; detailed source/snapshot metadata is conditional. This is model guidance, not hard truncation of saved responses.

Clarification widgets show their question title and answer choices without the description paragraph or repeated instructions to reply in chat. Clicking a choice immediately sends that answer through the normal guarded/idempotent chat send flow; do not fill or focus the composer. Preserve any existing draft text and attachments, and do not attach them to the choice. Disable choices while sending, running or applying a proposal; errors remain visible and allow retry. Preserve the answer callbacks, disabled states and warning messages. The design-system specimen uses this same production renderer.
