# Manrisk design system

## Risk creation clarity

Use RiskCreationContext and RiskFinalizationReview on new and draft risk forms. Unit defaults to the signed-in account. In Identifikasi Risiko, Pemilik Risiko defaults to the active creator in the owning unit on a new risk form. The field is optional and uses the same assignee presentation of RemoteUserPicker as the mitigation PIC, filtered to active users in the owning unit. Show the saved name in read-only views and offer Kosongkan pemilik risiko inside the dropdown while editing. Sumber Risiko offers Internal, External, and one combined External & Internal option for a risk with both sources. Place the Status draf risiko Alert at the top of the trailing rail above Properti. On narrower screens, show it above the form fields. Use variant info and a leading AlertCircle icon. Show live feedback when a draft is saving, saved, or failed to save. Use the blue notice tokens for light and dark themes. Initial profiles take the current Jakarta quarter at finalization, including drafts started in an earlier quarter. Preserve established version and monitoring periods. Show a 16px completion dot beside every archive section title. Clicking the active tab collapses its panel; all panels may be closed. Lift a hovered or keyboard-focused archive tab upward by 8px without changing stack layout; move its side border by the same amount so the outline stays connected. The first tab lifts 12px to keep its title below the topbar. Keep a white ring and neutral center while a section is incomplete, then light the center green when all required inputs in that section pass the same validation used for finalization. Approval is complete only when any capability-required reviewer and approval-line selections are present. Include completion state in each tab’s accessible name. Scores and residual targets begin unassessed, with no preselected values. Preserve older saved draft scores for review. Deskripsi Kejadian Risiko is optional for draft saving and finalization. Saving a draft requires title and category and preserves unassessed scores. Show saving, saved and retryable failure states. Guard navigation with save-and-leave, keep-editing and discard options. Finalization review shows period, score/level, residual-target, treatment-plan count and consequences. Format score and residual target as rounded integers through the shared formatRiskScore helper. Post-finalization feedback uses a blue Alert with variant info and a leading AlertCircle icon. It shows actual treatment-task availability and guidance for dated monitoring, with the action wrapping below the message on narrow screens. Keep loading, failure and empty task states distinct. The design-system catalog renders the production guidance and score controls.
